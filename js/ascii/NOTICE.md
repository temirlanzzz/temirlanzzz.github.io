# ASCII pieces

`runtime/` and `lib/` are copied unchanged from [ascii-magic-art](https://www.npmjs.com/package/ascii-magic-art) 1.0.2
by ASCII Magic (ascii-magic.com), MIT License (see LICENSE).

`pieces/neural-forward-pass.js` is adapted from that package's piece of the same name
(site palette in both themes, labels corrected). `pieces/sunk-404.js`, `pieces/career-log.js`
and `pieces/reel-loader.js` are written for this site in the package's piece format;
`sunk-404` takes its block digits from the package's `page-404` piece and `career-log` its layout
from `git-log-graph`.

Nothing here loads anything from ascii-magic.com: every file is served from this site.
