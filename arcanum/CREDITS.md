# Credits

Arcanum's products are real scanned or hand-modelled 3D assets from the
[Khronos glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets) collection.
Each model is used under the licence its authors chose (CC0-1.0 or CC-BY-4.0). Models were
re-compressed for the web (meshopt geometry, WebP textures at 512–1024 px) with
[glTF-Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev); geometry and materials are otherwise unchanged.
Product names, prices and descriptions in the shop are fictional.

## Textures

- `public/tex/wood_*.webp` — `hardwood2` textures from the [three.js examples](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures) (MIT).
- `public/tex/fabric_*.png` — technical fabric maps from the Khronos `SheenCloth` sample (CC0-1.0, Microsoft).
- `public/tex/venice_sunset_1k.jpg` — a crop of the `venice_sunset_1k` HDRI from the three.js examples
  (originally by Poly Haven, CC0), used as the street view outside the doors.

Everything else (the building, books, rugs, signs, price tags and artwork) is generated in code.
