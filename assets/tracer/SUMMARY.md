**Provenance Tracer** finds where a piece of text came from (earliest source, exact copies, near-duplicates, paraphrases). Every conclusion comes with evidence you can check yourself: highlighted matched spans, similarity bars, and publish or archive dates.
**Stack:** Python/FastAPI · web search via Brave, SerpAPI, DuckDuckGo or Bing · trafilatura + htmldate · 5-gram shingles/MinHash + MiniLM sentence embeddings · Wayback Machine CDX first-seen dates · imagehash/EXIF/C2PA for images · vanilla JS UI with light/dark mode · Docker · 15 pytest tests.
**Real run (Pride & Prejudice, ch. 1):** checked 12 live pages and found 2 exact copies and 3 near-duplicates. It dated the origin to pemberley.com, first archived 1998-12-02; page metadata alone pointed to a 2024 page. 37 s cold, about 6 s with the Wayback cache warm.
