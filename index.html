Hi, I’m Temirlan. I make machine learning boring enough to trust.

I’m a software engineer from Kazakhstan. I spent the last few years in Germany: a master’s in data analytics in Hildesheim, then ML pipelines for robots that handle textiles at Sewts in Munich. Now I’m back in Almaty, leading the engineering team at Adigy.ai, where we manage Amazon ads.

I got hooked on building things through school competitions in programming and robotics. My favourite project was a smart vest for visually impaired people, which won first place at a national project competition. At Nazarbayev University I tutored other students in programming and published my first ML papers.

The part I enjoy most comes after the demo works: making it fast, cheap and measurable. Off the clock I read about robotics, driver-assistance systems and startups, and build small ML tools like the ones below.
